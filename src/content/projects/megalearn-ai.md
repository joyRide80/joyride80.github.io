## Role & Strategy

I co-founded Megalearn and designed and built the system, including agent-based tools for producing game assets and lesson content. The work combines product design, game design, and front-end development in TypeScript and Three.js.

- **Text-to-voxel assets:** I built a pipeline that turns text prompts into 3D voxel models, reducing the manual work needed to populate each game world.

![|span:3|col:2](/images/projects/megalearn-ai/cuteVoxel.mp4)

- **Procedural worlds:** I designed a modular terrain system that generates environments from parameters such as humidity and temperature.

![|span:2|col:1](/images/projects/megalearn-ai/terrainEditor-shape.png)

![|span:1|col:3](/images/projects/megalearn-ai/terrainEditor-climate.png)

![|span:1|col:4](/images/projects/megalearn-ai/terrainEditor-surface.png)

- **Real-code prototypes:** I tested the Hypothesis, Experiment, and Conclusion loop in working prototypes, then refined the MVP with teachers and parents.

![|span:1|col:1](/images/projects/megalearn-ai/mission-overlay.png)

![|span:2|col:2](/images/projects/megalearn-ai/writing.png)

- **Responsive dialogue:** Conversation scenes use AI-generated emotion tags to change character expressions and the atmosphere around each exchange.

![|span:2|col:1](/images/projects/megalearn-ai/conversation1.png)

![|span:2|col:3](/images/projects/megalearn-ai/conversation2.png)
